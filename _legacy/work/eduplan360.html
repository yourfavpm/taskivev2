Overview

International students face one of the most fragmented journeys in any service category: course selection, school selection, document prep, and visa approval, often spread across unrelated tools and advisors. EduPlan360 needed one coherent platform for the whole journey.

The problem

Coordinating course selection, school applications, document verification, and visa support across dozens of destination countries and university partners is inherently complex — and any gap in that chain can cost a student their visa timeline or their place at a school.

What we built

  • A destinations and university-partner content system covering dozens of countries and institutions.
  • A student portal for document submission, application tracking, and status updates in one place.
  • A consultation and advisory booking flow connecting students directly with counsellors.
  • An architecture built to operate across multiple regions and time zones reliably.
