Overview

Kemuko needed to build genuine demand and a founding community before the product itself was ready — turning a pre-launch period into an asset instead of dead time.

The problem

Launching cold, with no audience or signal of demand, is one of the highest-risk moments for a consumer product. Kemuko needed a way to capture interest early, reward the earliest members, and build momentum ahead of launch day.

What we built

  • A waitlist site designed to convert visitors into signed-up early members quickly.
  • Early-access growth mechanics: early-bird discounts, free courses, and rewards for joining ahead of launch.
  • A lightweight, fast-loading experience built to handle a traffic spike from a single campaign or share.
