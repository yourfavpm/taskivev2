Overview

Service businesses needed more than a booking form. They needed a way to launch a branded booking website quickly, then run bookings, payments, scheduling, client records, and staff operations from a single platform.

The problem

Most tools solved only one part of the workflow. Owners had to stitch together booking links, calendars, payment tools, CRM software, and staff scheduling, which created friction for both the business and the customer.

What we built

  • A booking website builder that lets a service business launch a branded site in minutes, not weeks.
  • A scheduling system that handles availability, time zones, rescheduling, and reminders without manual coordination.
  • Integrated payments so bookings can be confirmed and paid for in one flow.
  • CRM and staff tools so owners can manage clients, team members, and day-to-day operations in one dashboard.
